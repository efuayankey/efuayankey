## Hey, I’m Efua!

I’m a Computer Science & Engineering student at Lehigh University interested in backend engineering, distributed systems, infrastructure, and AI.

A lot of what I build starts with either a problem I want to solve or something I’m curious enough to understand by building it myself. That’s taken me from student-facing products and AI tools to distributed task processing, monitoring systems, and backend infrastructure.

I recently worked as a Software Engineering Intern at PrePass, where I contributed to the backend and infrastructure for a real-time safety alert system. I’m also doing neurosymbolic AI research at Lehigh, exploring how neural models can produce explanations that are easier to understand as logical rules.

Lately, I’ve been especially interested in the systems behind a product — how services communicate, scale, recover from failure, and stay reliable when people actually depend on them — while continuing to explore AI alongside that.

Outside of engineering, I care a lot about mentorship, representation, and building stronger technical communities. I’m also usually listening to music, taking photos, or watching more basketball than I probably should.

I’m always happy to talk about interesting engineering problems, research, projects, or opportunities to build something useful.
